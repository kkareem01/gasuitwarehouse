Suit Alterations & Tailoring at GA Suit Warehouse.

Most alteration shops in Hall County quote you a single number — $120 for the suit — without explaining what that includes or what it doesn't. We do the opposite. Every adjustment is line-item priced. Pant hem $18. Waist taper $24. Sleeve shortening $25 to $45 depending on whether the buttonholes need to be moved. Jacket sides $40 to $80. Lining work and structural repairs quoted on inspection. A typical full-suit alteration — hem, waist, sleeves, and sides on a two-piece — runs $60 to $135. You see each cost before our tailors touch the suit, and you only pay for the work you actually need. Bring in a single pair of pants for a hem and that's $18, not a $50 minimum.

The honest part of this conversation is what can't be altered. Shoulders are effectively impossible. Technically a tailor can rebuild the armhole and reshape the canvas, but it's a 4-to-6-hour job that runs $150-$300 and rarely produces a clean result. If your shoulders don't fit, the right answer is a different jacket, not surgery on the one you have. Side seams give you about an inch in either direction. Most suits carry 1 to 1.5 inches of fabric in the seams and waist; past that, the balance breaks down and the suit reads off. Pants waists are slightly more forgiving (up to 2 inches in many cases) but rise and seat depth are nearly impossible to change. Bring the suit in and we'll measure the existing seam allowance for free before we quote any work, so you know up front what's possible.

We alter suits regardless of where you bought them. Suitsupply, Indochino, Men's Wearhouse, an old Brooks Brothers handed down from your father — we treat each one the same. Standard turnaround is 3 to 5 business days for a typical hem-waist-sleeves combo, 1 to 2 days for a single hem, and 5 to 7 days for full restructure work. Our $10 same-day rush moves you to the front of the queue and covers low-risk work — pant hems, waist tapers, sleeve length on certain constructions. It does not cover jacket sides or shoulder work, because those need cure time and pressing. Walk in by 11am and rush work is ready by close. We built this option for the Gainesville customer who got 24-hour notice on a wedding, an interview, or a service. If your alterations would run more than $200 on a $300 suit, we'll show you the math on a $399 floor suit instead — sometimes replacing is the smarter call.
