## Important notes

- **Export both formats.** Site uses `<picture>` tags — every photo needs `name.jpg` AND `name.webp`. Skip the webp and it falls back unstyled on slow connections.
- **Shoot detail + full-body for each look.** Hero areas use full-body; spoke cards crop tight. Same shoot, two crops.
- **Backdrop:** clean gray/white inside the store for product shots; storefront or venue corner for the group/event shots (prom group, gala tux, wedding party).
- **If doing a partial shoot, prioritize shoes (15-17), accessories (12-14), and alterations (18-20).** Those three pages have ZERO matching imagery today — every card is a fabric swatch or generic model.
- **Worst-offender images to replace:** `Modelleft.jpg`, `modelright.jpg`, `right-fit.jpg`, `fabric-swatches-banner.jpg` — each currently used in 5+ unrelated contexts.
- **Group shots can be staff/family.** No need for paid models for the groomsmen (#6, #7) or prom group (#10) — they read better as real people anyway.
