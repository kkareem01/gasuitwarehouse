What we do

Bowties at GA Suit Warehouse.

Bowties exist in three contexts: formal (black silk for tuxedos), wedding (color and patterned for grooms and groomsmen), and personal style (the guy who wears bowties daily as a signature). Each context has different rules and different right-answers. We stock bowties for all three: formal black silk in pre-tied and self-tie, wedding bowties across 24 colors and 50 patterns, and signature-style bowties in cotton, knit, wool, and novelty fabrics.

Pre-tied vs self-tie. Pre-tied bowties are pre-knotted with adjustable neck strap — easier, faster, never come undone, look slightly less natural in photos. Self-tie bowties require tying like a shoe — more authentic, slightly more rumpled and human in photos, can come untied at the worst time. For weddings and formal events: self-tie reads more sophisticated. For prom and pre-tied looks: pre-tied is fine and easier. Clip-on bowties are appropriate only for kids; adult clip-ons read off. Black silk bowtie is the only correct tie with a black tuxedo at a formal event. Color bowties (burgundy, navy, hunter green, blush) work for weddings, prom, and personal style. Patterned bowties (paisley, dot, plaid) work for casual smart-dressing and signature style. Sizes mostly one-size-fits-all; XL bowties available for larger neck circumference.
