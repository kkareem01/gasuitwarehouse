Privacy Policy

Effective date: April 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what GA Suit Warehouse, LLC ("we", "us", "our") collects when you visit gasuitwarehouse.com or book a fitting with us, how we use it, and the choices you have.

What we collect

When you book a fitting through our website or by phone, we collect the information you provide directly: your name, phone number, email address, the occasion you're shopping for, and the date and time you'd like to come in.

When you visit our website, we automatically collect basic technical information through privacy-friendly analytics (Vercel Analytics): the pages you view, your approximate location (city/region only), the device type, and the referring page. We do not use cookies for advertising, and we do not sell or share your information with advertising networks.

How we use it

We use the information you provide to schedule and confirm your fitting, to contact you about your appointment, and to follow up on your visit. We use site analytics in aggregate to understand which pages are useful and to improve the site.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share it with third parties for marketing purposes.

Service providers

We use a small number of vendors to operate the site and the booking system, including Vercel (hosting and analytics) and an email/SMS provider for confirmation messages. These vendors only process information on our behalf and only as needed to provide their service.

Your choices

You may ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you. You may unsubscribe from booking-related messages at any time by replying to the message or calling the shop. To make a request, contact us using the information below.

Children

The website is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "effective date" above will reflect the most recent change.
